    Questions

    Common questions

    What is water-fed pole window cleaning?

    It's a method that uses purified water and extendable poles to clean glass from the ground. Because the water is filtered, it dries clear with no mineral spots or streaks. It's safer than ladders and lets us rinse frames and edges thoroughly, which is ideal for multi-story homes and commercial buildings.

    Will pressure washing damage my siding?

    High pressure can, which is why we soft wash siding, roofs and awnings. A cleaning solution does the work and we rinse at low pressure. Concrete and other hard surfaces get full pressure because they can handle it.

    How often should I have my windows and house cleaned?

    Near the water, salt and humidity build up faster. Many coastal homeowners clean windows a few times a year and wash the house about once a year. We'll recommend a schedule based on your home.

    Is the online estimate my final price?

    It's a range for a typical home. Jeff confirms your exact price before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

    Do you work on commercial properties?

    Yes. We maintain storefronts, offices, restaurants and other commercial spaces, and we schedule around your business hours.

    What if I'm not happy with the job?

    Call or text us. If you aren't thrilled, we'll do everything in our power to make it right. Satisfaction is guaranteed.
