Good to know

Straight answers.

Have a different question? Text (757) 749-9274 and a real person will answer.

Is the price on the website really the price?

Yes, for typical homes. The quote box prices by surfaces, stories and size. We confirm it at the walkthrough before we start. If something is unusual, like a three-story rear or a huge paver patio, we tell you then, never on the invoice.

Will pressure washing damage my siding?

High pressure can. That's why siding and roofs get soft washed: a cleaning solution does the work and we rinse at about garden-hose pressure. Concrete and some wood get real pressure because they can take it.

What is water-fed pole window cleaning?

It's a modern method that uses purified water and extendable poles to clean glass safely from the ground. Because the water is filtered, it dries clear without mineral spots or streaks. It also cuts down on ladders and lets us rinse frames and edges thoroughly, which works well for multi-story homes and commercial buildings.

Can you take care of my business property?

Yes. We clean storefronts, office buildings, restaurants and other commercial spaces throughout Hampton Roads, and we schedule around your hours to keep disruption to a minimum.

Do you clean windows inside too?

Our standard window service covers exterior glass, screens, sills and tracks. We clean interior glass on request and quote it at the walkthrough.

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need the water spigot on and the gate unlocked. We text when we arrive and send before and after photos when we're done.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

The mix is biodegradable, and we pre-wet, cover and rinse all your plants. Keep pets inside while we work and for about an hour after.

How long does it last?

A house wash near the water usually holds 12 to 18 months, longer inland. Roofs last 3 to 5 years. Windows near the coast look best cleaned two to four times a year. Priority Members stay on a regular schedule at a locked rate.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Call or text us. If you aren't thrilled with the work, we'll do everything in our power to make it right. Satisfaction is guaranteed.

Do you offer coupons?

Yes. First-time customers get $25 off any one service, and when you refer a friend you get $30 off your next service. Mention the coupon when you book.

How do I pay?

Card on file or text-to-pay, charged after the walkthrough. Nothing is charged when you book.
