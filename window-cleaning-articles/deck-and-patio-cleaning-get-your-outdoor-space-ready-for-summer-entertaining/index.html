Why Clean Your Deck and Patio Before Summer

Our coastal climate speeds up buildup. Moisture invites algae, pollen sticks to it, and traffic tracks in sand that scuffs and stains. That combination leaves dark patches, slippery areas, and a tired look across concrete patios, pavers, and composite or wood decks. A professional cleaning brightens every board and slab so your space is ready for guests, photos, and long evenings outside.

  • Salt spray and humidity encourage algae that makes surfaces slick.
  • Pollen and dust settle into porous materials and darken the finish.
  • Grill grease, food spills, and drink splashes leave stubborn rings and shadows.

Wet, shady patios grow slippery mildew fast. Cleaning before the first big BBQ helps prevent slips on steps, landings, and poolside areas when friends arrive.
