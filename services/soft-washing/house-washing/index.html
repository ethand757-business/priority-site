What House Washing Is and Why It Matters

Unlike high-pressure blasting, soft washing relies on low water pressure and targeted detergents to lift and rinse away organic growth. This method helps prevent water intrusion behind siding, avoids etching delicate surfaces, and keeps seals around windows and doors intact. Done regularly, it reduces the chance of stains setting in and keeps maintenance simple.

Many homeowners group exterior care on the same day for a smoother, faster visit. If your downspouts are overflowing or you see streaks after rain, consider adding gutter cleaning to keep stormwater moving away from the foundation. Dark streaks on shingles can often be treated safely with non-pressure methods as part of professional roof cleaning.

Our Safe Soft Washing Process

From the first hello to the final rinse, we keep the process straightforward, careful, and respectful of your property. Soft washing is designed to clean exterior surfaces thoroughly without the risks that can come with high-pressure methods. Our approach focuses on proper preparation, the right cleaning blends, and controlled application to deliver consistent results across siding, trim, and other exterior materials. Here is how a typical appointment with Priority Window Cleaning goes:

  1. Walkthrough and protection: We begin with a detailed walkthrough of your home's exterior. During this step, we inspect siding, trim, soffits, and other surfaces to identify areas with heavier buildup or staining. We also point out any pre-existing concerns so expectations are clear from the start. Before cleaning begins, we take steps to protect delicate plants, outdoor electronics, and nearby furnishings as needed.
  2. Solution application: Next, we apply a professional-grade, gentle cleaning blend designed to break down algae, mildew, and embedded dirt. This solution does the heavy lifting by loosening organic growth at the root level so it can be rinsed away effectively. The application is even and controlled to ensure consistent coverage without oversaturation.
  3. Low-pressure rinse: After the solution has had time to work, we perform a controlled, low-pressure rinse from top to bottom. This method safely removes residues without damaging siding, paint, or caulking. The result is a cleaner, brighter exterior without the risks associated with excessive pressure.
  4. Quality check: We finish with a final review of the cleaned areas. If any spots need additional attention, we address them promptly. We also ensure pathways, entry points, and surrounding areas are left neat and orderly before we consider the job complete.

Common questions

How Often Should I Schedule House Washing?

Most homes benefit from a professional cleaning once a year, though some coastal properties with heavy shade or moisture may prefer every 9 to 12 months. Frequency can vary by siding type, tree cover, and exposure to salt air. We will help you find a schedule that balances appearance, budget, and long-term care.

Will Soft Washing Damage My Plants or Paint?

We protect landscaping before we begin and use a low-pressure rinse that is safer for paint, caulk, and trim. Our soaps are diluted to the right strength for cleaning power without unnecessary harshness, and we control runoff carefully. If we see sensitive areas during the walkthrough, we add extra rinsing and coverings for peace of mind.

What Should I Do Before the Crew Arrives?

Please close windows, move cars from the driveway, and bring in small items like cushions or yard decor. If you have pets, we recommend keeping them inside until the final rinse is complete. We will handle the rest, including a careful post-clean check before we leave.
