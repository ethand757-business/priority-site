What Is Soft Washing?

Soft washing is a safe, low-pressure cleaning method that removes organic growth like algae, mildew, and grime using eco-conscious detergents and a gentle rinse. Unlike high-pressure methods, it will not force water under siding, erode mortar, or strip protective granules from shingles. This approach is ideal for vinyl, painted wood, stucco, composite siding, and asphalt roofs where care matters most. When you choose Priority Window Cleaning, you get a thoughtful process, reliable scheduling, and helpful guidance on keeping your home looking great between visits.

Benefits of Soft Washing for Your Home

  • Protects siding, shingles, trim, and seals by using low-pressure and targeted solutions.
  • Removes algae, mold, mildew, and stains for a cleaner, brighter curb appeal.
  • Helps surfaces last longer by clearing contaminants that break down coatings.
  • Improves health and comfort by reducing allergens around entryways and patios.
  • Prepares surfaces for painting or maintenance by creating a clean, ready-to-bond surface.

How Long Do Soft Washing Results Last?

Results typically last 12 to 24 months, depending on shade, trees, and exposure to salt air. Siding with heavy shade or north-facing walls may need attention sooner. We are happy to recommend a refresh schedule based on your materials and setting. With simple upkeep, many homes can maintain a fresh look well past a year.

Is Soft Washing Safe for My Plants and Pets?

Yes. We protect landscaping before we begin, use controlled application, and rinse thoroughly. We also encourage light watering after service. Pets can enjoy the yard again once surfaces are dry, usually later the same day.

What Makes Soft Washing Different From Pressure Washing?

Soft washing relies on specialized detergents to break down organic growth, while pressure washing relies mostly on force. The low-pressure rinse in soft washing protects delicate surfaces like shingles, stucco, and painted wood. Pressure washing still has a place on durable materials like concrete and stone when used correctly. We will suggest the right method for each area so your home gets the safest, best result.
