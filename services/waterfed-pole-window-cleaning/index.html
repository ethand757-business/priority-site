What Water-Fed Pole Cleaning Is and How It Works

Water-fed pole window cleaning pairs a lightweight telescoping pole with a soft-bristle brush that agitates dirt while pure water rinses everything clean. Because the purified water has no minerals, it dries clear and streak-free on its own. This approach is ideal for delicate glass, painted frames, and modern low-e windows because it avoids harsh detergents and aggressive scraping.

We tailor the technique to your property. For example, larger panes near trees may need extra agitation, while shaded sides of the home often hold onto mildew and require a slower rinse. Our trained technicians read the glass and surrounding surfaces so every pass is efficient and gentle.

Our Step-By-Step Window Washing Process

We follow a simple, consistent method so your results are predictable and spotless every time:

  1. Walkthrough: Note window locations, screens, and surface conditions.
  2. Prep: Move light obstacles, protect nearby fixtures, and set up cones if needed.
  3. Brush and Rinse: Agitate dirt with a soft brush while feeding pure water through the pole.
  4. Detail Work: Rinse frames and sills so edges dry cleanly and evenly.
  5. Final Check: Inspect from multiple angles to ensure a streak-free dry down.

This proven process delivers a consistent finish across your property. It also pairs well with routine maintenance like house washing to keep siding and trim looking fresh.

Will Water-Fed Poles Scratch My Glass or Frames?

No. The brushes are soft and designed to lift dirt while the pure water rinses it away. We avoid metal tools on glass during this process. Frames and sills are gently rinsed so everything dries clean without streaks.

How Often Should I Schedule Service Near the Coast?

Frequency varies with wind, pollen, and proximity to the ocean. Many properties thrive with quarterly visits, while storefronts on busy roads may benefit from monthly rinses. We will help you choose a schedule that matches your location and budget.

Is Water-Fed Pole Cleaning Effective on Second- and Third-Story Windows?

Yes. Telescoping poles reach upper windows from the ground, which improves safety and saves setup time. On exceptionally high panes or complex architecture, we combine methods to ensure a spotless, consistent finish.
