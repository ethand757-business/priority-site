Custom Christmas Light Installation That Brings Your Vision to Life

Your home should look magical, not mismatched. Our team starts with a quick conversation about your goals, color preferences, and the features you want to highlight. We measure rooflines, peaks, railings, and landscape elements so your Christmas lights look balanced from every angle. Whether you prefer warm white elegance or colorful, playful patterns, we'll plan a design that feels made for your space.

We use commercial-grade LED strands, pro-grade clips, and weather-resistant timers so your system is safe and efficient. The result is a curated display that glows beautifully at night and looks tidy in the daytime. If you want wreaths, garland, stake lights, or lit greenery, we'll incorporate those pieces for a polished finish. Best of all, you won't climb a ladder or spend hours untangling cords.

As the area's go-to for exterior care, our team is also trusted as Virginia Beach's window washing experts when your glass needs attention during the season. Many homeowners schedule both services together to get that picture-perfect holiday sparkle from the curb to the living room view.

How Our Holiday Lighting Process Works

Our process is simple and stress-free from start to finish. We handle design, materials, installation, maintenance, takedown, and storage, so you enjoy the season without the hassle.

  1. Design Chat: Share your ideas and budget. We recommend colors, bulb types, and placements that suit your home.
  2. On-Site Walkthrough: We measure key areas and confirm details like timers and special accents.
  3. Professional Installation: Trained technicians install lights safely and cleanly, protecting your property.
  4. Seasonal Support: If a bulb goes out, call us. We'll make it right quickly.
  5. Takedown and Storage: After the holidays, we remove lights, label them, and store them neatly for next year.

Local Expertise for Coastal Weather and Styles

Coastal air and seasonal wind call for smart product choices and careful fastening. We use clips and attachment methods that secure lights while protecting your roof and gutters. Our LED selections are rated for damp environments and stay bright without excess heat or power draw. You'll love how your display complements the natural beauty of Virginia Beach at night.

Local tip: Coastal breezes can shift decorations if they aren't secured correctly. Choosing pro-grade clips and balanced layouts helps your lights stay straight and beautiful all season.

Safety, Quality, and Maintenance You Can Count On

Your safety is our top priority. Our technicians use proper ladders, stabilizers, and fall-protection practices so you never need to climb. Cords are managed neatly, and timers keep everything on schedule. If anything needs attention during the season, we provide quick maintenance to keep your home glowing without interruption.

Many homeowners pair their display with other exterior cleaning solutions before installation. If your siding or walkways need a refresh, ask about related services like house washing. If windows need a spotless, streak-free finish to complement your lights, we also offer water-fed pole window cleaning that keeps glass crystal clear for those cozy indoor views of your display.

Design Ideas to Match Your Home

Every property is different, so we help you choose a style that suits your architecture and landscaping. A simple roofline with warm white bulbs creates an elegant frame. Tree wraps add depth and sparkle, and pathway stakes guide guests to your front door. For a bolder look, mix rooflines with wreaths, garland, and color pops around dormers and archways.

  • Classic Warm White: Timeless roofline glow with matching wreath and bows
  • Coastal Cool: Crisp white and blue lights with palm or pine wraps
  • Color Pop: Multicolor strands on trees with warm white roof highlights
  • Symmetry First: Balanced peaks and columns for a clean, upscale look

Not sure which option fits? We'll mock up ideas and help you pick a look that feels festive and fits your budget.

Common questions

How Long Does Installation Take?

Most standard displays are completed in a single visit, often within a few hours, depending on home size and design complexity. Larger homes or more detailed layouts with trees, wreaths, and multiple rooflines can take longer. We schedule enough time to do it safely and neatly, so your home looks perfect as soon as the sun sets.

Do You Provide the Lights, Or Do I Need To Buy Them?

We provide commercial-grade LEDs, clips, cords, and timers as part of your package, so you don't have to shop or guess what will work. These products are brighter, more durable, and more efficient than typical retail strands. We also handle any mid-season service, then take everything down and store it in an organized way for next year.

What If A Bulb Goes Out After Installation?

If a bulb fails or a section needs adjustment, just call us, and we'll schedule a quick visit. Our seasonal support covers common issues, so your display keeps shining. You can relax knowing a friendly local team is ready to help throughout the holidays.
