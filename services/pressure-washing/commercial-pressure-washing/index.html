How Often Should a Business Schedule Exterior Cleaning?

Most properties benefit from quarterly service, while high-traffic sites near the Oceanfront or busy shopping areas may prefer monthly touch-ups. Frequency varies by material, shade, trees, and foot traffic. We can assess your surfaces and recommend a plan that fits your goals and budget.

Will Pressure Washing Damage Paint or Signage?

Not when it is done correctly. We match pressure, tips, and cleaning solutions to the surface and use soft washing for delicate materials. That way, buildup is removed while paint, decals, and protective coatings stay intact.

Can You Work Before We Open or After We Close?

Yes. We offer flexible scheduling to minimize impact on business hours and customer flow. Let us know your preferred window, and we will plan accordingly, including safety cones and clear walkways for early arrivals.
