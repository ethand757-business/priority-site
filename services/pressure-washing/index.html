What Is Professional Pressure Washing?

It is a targeted exterior cleaning method that uses calibrated water pressure and detergents to lift grime, algae, and stains from hard surfaces. Our trained technicians match nozzle tips, flow rates, and soaps to the material so your property gets a deep clean without damage. Many surfaces need a soft wash approach that relies on lower pressure and dwell time rather than brute force. We combine equipment know-how with safe solutions to leave your home or business fresh and protected.

House Washing and Siding Care

Vinyl, fiber cement, and painted wood siding collect salt, mildew, and pollen that dull your color over time. Our gentle soft wash uses low pressure and biodegradable detergents to clean siding, trim, and soffits while protecting caulk and paint. Windows, landscaping, and outdoor fixtures are wetted and rinsed to keep them safe during the process. You will notice brighter siding, cleaner trim lines, and a fresher look from the street.

Deck and Fence Cleaning

Wood and composite boards can fade or grow algae in our humid climate, especially in shaded areas. We test a small area, then apply the right detergent to lift grime before a careful rinse that respects grain and coatings. Railings, posts, and stairs get the same level of detail, so the entire area feels renewed. If you plan to seal or stain, a clean surface helps finishes bond better and last longer.

Common questions

How Often Should I Clean My Driveway Or Siding?

Most homes benefit from an exterior clean once a year, with high-shade or high-traffic areas needing touch-ups sooner. Close to the water, some properties prefer a spring refresh and a late-summer maintenance visit. Timing can vary by material, tree cover, and exposure to salt spray. We are happy to recommend a schedule after a quick site check.

Will The Cleaning Hurt My Plants Or Paint?

Our team pre-wets vegetation, uses plant-safe detergents at proper dilution, and rinses thoroughly. For painted or delicate surfaces, we use soft wash settings and increase dwell time instead of force. We also test a small, hidden area before proceeding on the full surface. The goal is a safer, longer-lasting clean without damage.

How Long Does A Typical Visit Take?

Most single-family homes take a few hours, while larger properties and commercial sites can run longer. Access to water, number of surfaces, and stain severity all affect timing. We will provide a time window when scheduling and keep you updated on the day of service. If the weather shifts, we will coordinate a quick reschedule to keep your project on track.
