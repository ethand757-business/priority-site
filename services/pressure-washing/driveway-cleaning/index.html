Driveway cleaning is the targeted removal of contaminants from concrete, asphalt, pavers, and aggregate using specialized detergents and calibrated pressure. Common questions

How Often Should I Schedule Driveway Cleaning?

Most homes in Virginia Beach benefit from driveway cleaning once a year. Shaded or high-traffic driveways may need service twice yearly to keep algae and oil from setting in.

Will Pressure Washing Damage My Concrete or Pavers?

Not when done correctly. We use calibrated pressure and professional surface cleaners so the wash is even and gentle on your surface. We also pretreat stains so the tool does not have to work as hard.

How Long Before I Can Park on the Driveway Again?

In most cases, you can use the driveway as soon as it is dry, typically within a few hours. If we apply a sealer at your request, we will advise on specific cure times before we leave.
