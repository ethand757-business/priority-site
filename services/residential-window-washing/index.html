What Residential Window Washing Is and Why It Matters

Residential window washing is a structured cleaning service that removes salt film, pollen, grime, and stubborn spots from glass, frames, and sills. Clean windows let in more natural light, protect glass from etching, and make your home look cared for from the street. Done correctly, it also helps extend the life of your screens, seals, and exterior finishes.

  • Brighter rooms and better views for everyday living
  • Protection against mineral stains and salt corrosion
  • Safer results thanks to proper ladders, poles, and techniques
  • Time savings for busy families and seasonal residents
  • Flexible plans that fit your cleaning routine and budget

We design services that fit the way you use your home. Some homeowners prefer a light interior-only polish before the holidays. Others choose full inside-and-out care for every window, transom, and glass door each season.

Our Residential Window Washing Process

Your time and home are important, so our process is clear and consistent from start to finish.

  1. Walkthrough and protection: We confirm the scope, place drop cloths as needed, and move small items with care.
  2. Screen and sill care: We remove screens, brush dust and pollen, and wipe tracks and sills.
  3. Spot-safe wash: We pre-treat tough spots like sap or hard water, then wash with the right solution for your glass and frames.
  4. Streak-free finish: Professional squeegees and clean towels leave edges sharp and glass clear.
  5. Final check: We invite you to review results and make sure every pane meets our standards.

Pro tip for coastal living: Salt film builds faster on windward sides of homes near the water. Scheduling exterior residential window washing every 3-4 months on those sides helps prevent etching and keeps views bright year-round.

Our technicians arrive on time, wear clean uniforms, and communicate clearly. If a pane needs extra attention, we take care of it before we pack up. Your satisfaction matters to us, and we stand behind our work.

Common questions

How Often Should I Schedule Residential Window Washing Near the Coast?

For most homes in coastal areas, exterior windows benefit from service every 3-4 months because of salt and pollen. Interiors typically stay clean longer and can be done every 6-12 months, depending on pets, cooking, and dust. We can set up a simple schedule that focuses more often on the windward sides of your home and less often on the leeward sides, where glass stays cleaner.

What Do I Need to Do Before the Crew Arrives?

We ask that you clear small items from window sills and unlock gates for safe access. Our team brings coverings for floors and furniture, and we move lightweight items as needed. If you have specific requests, tell us during the walkthrough so we can plan the best approach for your home.

Will Professional Cleaning Scratch My Glass or Damage My Frames?

No. We use soft scrub pads, clean towels, and solutions that match your window materials. Our tools and methods are designed to remove grime without scratching glass or harming seals and finishes. If we see a risk area, we point it out first and adjust our process to protect it.
