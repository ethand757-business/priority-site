Common questions

How often should I schedule window cleaning?

Most homes look their best with service two to four times per year. Homes near trees or high-traffic roads may prefer a more frequent schedule to keep dust and pollen under control.

Will cleaning scratch or wear down my glass?

No. We use soft tools and approved solutions that lift dirt safely. Tough buildup is treated with care so glass stays clear and undamaged.

Do you clean screens and tracks?

Yes. Standard service includes light screen cleaning and a wipe of sills and tracks. If screens need extra care, we will let you know and offer options.
