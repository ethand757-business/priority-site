Our Proven Process for Sparkling Windows

Clean windows do not happen by accident. They require a consistent method, the right tools, and attention to the small details that make a noticeable difference. Our process is designed to be simple for you while delivering professional, streak-free results every time. From your first call to the final inspection, we focus on clear communication, careful preparation, and thorough workmanship so you know exactly what to expect.

  1. Friendly Estimate: Getting started is easy. You can share your window count, basic measurements, or even photos for fast, accurate pricing. We review the scope of work with you, answer any questions, and confirm scheduling details right away. There is no confusion about what is included, and we make sure expectations are clear before service day.
  2. On-Time Arrival: Punctuality matters. Our technicians arrive as scheduled, greet you professionally, and walk through the plan to ensure we are aligned. Before we begin, we take steps to protect interior floors, nearby furnishings, and exterior landscaping. We set up ladders and equipment with safety in mind, keeping pathways organized and minimizing disruption to your home or business.
  3. Professional Cleaning: We start by detailing frames, sills, and tracks to remove dust and debris that can affect both appearance and window function. The glass is then carefully scrubbed to loosen dirt, pollen, and residue. Using professional-grade squeegees and tools, we remove water and cleaning solution evenly to achieve a clear, streak-free finish. Screens are rinsed and cleaned separately so they do not transfer dirt back onto freshly washed glass.
  4. Final Walk-Through: Before packing up, we inspect every pane from multiple angles and in different lighting conditions. If we notice any remaining spots or streaks, we correct them immediately. We review the completed work with you to ensure satisfaction and leave the area tidy and ready to enjoy.

Questions

Common questions

How do you protect interior spaces?

We wear shoe covers, move or cover nearby items as needed, and use clean drop cloths under each work area.

Can you clean skylights and high windows?

Yes. We use safe ladders, stabilizers, and extendable poles. For very high glass, we use purified water-fed systems for a spot-free finish.

Will pressure cleaning damage siding or paint?

Not with our methods. We match the pressure and solution to the surface and always test in a small area before proceeding.

What if it rains on my service day?

Light rain usually does not affect results. If weather impacts quality or safety, we reschedule quickly at no cost.
