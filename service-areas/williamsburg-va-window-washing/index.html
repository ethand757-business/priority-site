Questions

Common questions

How often should I schedule glass care?

Most homes benefit from cleaning two to four times per year, with timing adjusted for trees, traffic dust, and weather. Businesses near busy roads may choose monthly or bi-monthly service for consistent storefront shine.

Do you clean screens, tracks, and skylights?

Yes. We offer screen brushing, deep track detailing, and safe skylight care. Let us know what you need so we can include it in your quote.

What about hard water spots?

We can often improve or remove mineral buildup with specialized pads and solutions. Severe etching may need restoration, and we will advise you before work begins.

Is pressure cleaning safe for my siding?

We use the correct pressure and detergents for each surface. On delicate materials, we rely on a soft-wash approach to clean thoroughly without forcing water where it should not go.
