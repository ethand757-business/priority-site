Common questions

How often should I schedule service?

Most residential properties benefit from twice-yearly window washing to maintain clarity and prevent buildup from bonding to the glass. In coastal or high-pollen areas, additional touch-ups may be helpful after heavy pollen seasons, tropical weather, or strong coastal storms that leave behind salt residue. Commercial properties, especially high-traffic storefronts and offices, often prefer quarterly or even monthly cleanings to maintain a polished, professional appearance for customers and tenants.

What about screens and tracks?

Windows are more than just glass, and we treat them that way. Our service includes a careful wipe-down of sills, frames, and tracks to remove dust, debris, and buildup that can affect operation over time. We also wash and rinse screens to clear away pollen and dirt, improving airflow and helping your windows look fully refreshed from edge to edge.

Will you move furniture or protect floors?

Yes. We take steps to protect your interior spaces during service. Drop cloths are placed where needed to safeguard flooring, and we carefully reposition lightweight furniture or décor with your permission. Our goal is to leave your home exactly as we found it, just brighter and cleaner.

Can I combine services?

Absolutely. Many clients choose to pair window cleaning with driveway, patio, or siding cleaning for a more complete exterior refresh. Bundling services saves time, reduces scheduling hassle, and ensures your entire property looks consistently maintained.
