Our Window Cleaning Process

Windows look best when the whole opening gets attention. Here is how our step-by-step process delivers a brilliant finish:

  1. Protect: Place drop cloths and corner guards where needed.
  2. Prep: Brush loose dirt from frames, tracks, and screens.
  3. Clean: Apply a safe, streak-free solution and scrub the glass.
  4. Rinse: Use purified water when appropriate for a spot-free rinse.
  5. Squeegee: Precision technique to prevent lines and drips.
  6. Detail: Wipe edges, frames, and sills for a polished look.
  7. Final Check: Walk-through with you before we pack up.

Local Tip: Coastal air can leave salt and fine sand on exterior glass. A seasonal cleaning schedule in spring and late summer helps protect seals and keeps views clear during busy months.
