Chesapeake Window Washing Services We Offer

Whether it's a cozy ranch home or a multi-story property with expansive glass, our window cleaning process is designed to deliver a clear, streak-free finish with careful attention to detail. We begin by evaluating the condition of the glass, frames, seals, and nearby landscaping to determine the safest and most effective approach. Different materials require different techniques, so we select cleaning solutions and tools that protect tinted glass, specialty coatings, and surrounding finishes. Throughout the service, we work methodically to minimize disruption to your home or business. At the end of your appointment, we complete a walk-through with you so you can review the results, enjoy the improved view, and ask questions about maintenance between cleanings.

Our services are tailored to both residential and light commercial properties, allowing homeowners and storefront managers to maintain a polished, professional appearance year-round. We address not only the glass itself but also the surrounding components that affect overall clarity and cleanliness.

  • Interior and exterior window washing for homes and storefronts
  • Screen, track, and sill detailing to reduce dust buildup and improve airflow
  • Spot treatment for hard-water marks where feasible
  • Skylight, patio door, and sunroom glass cleaning

If you have specialty glass, decorative panes, oversized windows, or unique architectural features, let us know when you call. We arrive prepared with the proper equipment and techniques to protect seals, frames, coatings, and nearby surfaces while delivering a consistently clean, professional result.

Your Chesapeake Pressure Washing Service for Tough Grime

Stubborn buildup on concrete, brick, and composite surfaces needs the right balance of pressure and flow. As your dependable Chesapeake pressure washing service, we match the cleaning method to the surface so you get a deep clean without damage. Driveways, walkways, patios, and pool decks are common areas that benefit from a fresh wash before spring entertaining or holiday guests.

Ask about pairing window cleaning with pressure washing on the same visit for a simple, cost-effective refresh. Many clients love how their whole property pops once the concrete and siding come clean. We also offer gentle options for delicate exteriors when high pressure is not the right fit.

Our Safe, Step-by-Step Cleaning Process

  1. Arrival and walkthrough to note fragile areas, landscaping, and access points
  2. Dry dusting of frames and screens to remove loose debris
  3. Application of a window-safe solution and gentle agitation where needed
  4. Pure-water rinse or squeegee finish for a crisp, streak-free look
  5. Final detailing of edges, sills, and touch points
  6. Post-clean walkthrough to confirm you love the result

For sensitive siding or roof materials, we use controlled techniques instead of high force. You can also combine exterior glass cleaning with soft washing for a complete curb-appeal boost in one appointment.

Local Tip: Pollen season in Hampton Roads can coat glass quickly. A spring clean followed by a light mid-season touch-up keeps views clear and reduces sticky buildup that is harder to remove later.

Common questions

How often should I schedule window service?

Most homes look their best with two to four visits per year. Coastal air, trees near the home, and traffic dust can increase the need for more frequent cleanings.

Do you move furniture or window treatments?

We handle most light items with care. If there are heavy pieces or specialty treatments, we will coordinate a safe plan before service begins.

What if it rains after my appointment?

Light rain rarely affects freshly cleaned windows. If anything needs attention, just call us. Your satisfaction matters to our team.

Are your methods safe for tinted or coated glass?

Yes. We select solutions and tools designed for modern glass, seals, and frames, and we test an out-of-the-way area when needed.
